## Description
The purpose of this repository is to reproduce Dragon Ball Z Buu's Fury from Game Boy Advance, i started the latter to improve my python coding skills firstly, and to remake one of my favorite childhood games from GBA


## Prerequisites
Install pip if it's not already installed and install the requirements using pip
`$ pip3 install -r requirements.txt`

Download the sprite sheets (they are not stored in this repository):
`$ GH_TOKEN=<token> ./setup.sh`

- The characters (Gohan, Vegeta and Trunks in base and Super Saiyan, Broly, Goku SSJ3) and the effects of Trunks' and Broly's techniques come from the private [dbz-assets](https://github.com/Chakib08/dbz-assets) repository. `GH_TOKEN` is a GitHub token that can read it, e.g. a fine-grained token with read-only **Contents** access to that repository. The script downloads its `assets.zip`, or the whole repository as an archive when there is no `assets.zip`.
- Goku base and SSJ, and the effects of the special techniques (blasts, beams, impacts, the SSJ3 aura), come from spritedatabase.net. The sheets used only for effects go in `Graphics/assets/effects/`.

It needs `curl`, and `unzip` or `python3`. Files already downloaded are skipped; use `./setup.sh --force` to download them again. An older setup with spritedatabase sheets where the new ones go is moved over automatically.

The sprite configs of the dbz-assets sheets (`Config/characters/<name>/<form>.json` and the technique poses in `specials.json`) are generated by `python3 tools/build_sprite_configs.py`. Those sheets share one layout of 32x32 cells, one row per direction; the columns of each character's moves are listed in that script.

Map collisions: rooms are closed (walls, furniture and the void around them block movement) and the river can only be crossed on the bridge. The boxes are generated into the maps by `tools/build_collisions.py`, which describes each room's walkable floor and furniture; after changing a map, update its floor there and run `python3 tools/build_collisions.py` (hand-made collision boxes in the maps are kept).

To find sprites yourself in a ROM dumped from your own cartridge, `tools/gba_rip.py` finds compressed graphics and renders them as browsable PNG tile sheets (`python3 tools/gba_rip.py --help`). Never commit the ROM or its output.

Application settings are stored in `Config/config.json`.

Launch the game
`cd Dragon-Ball-Z-Buu-s-Fury/`
`python3 src/main.py`

## Version

The version (shown in the bottom-left corner of the main menu, and used for the Android app) is in `src/version.py`. Change it there when tagging a release (e.g. `v0.0.6`); `buildozer.spec` reads it from that file.

## Android APK

The **Build Android APK** GitHub Actions workflow builds a debug APK on pushes to `main`, pull requests targeting `main`, and manual runs. Download the `android-apk` artifact from the completed workflow run. Pushing a version tag such as `v1.0.0` also attaches the APK to a GitHub Release.

To build locally, install [Buildozer](https://buildozer.readthedocs.io/) and run:

```sh
GH_TOKEN=<token> ./setup.sh
buildozer android debug
```

The workflow downloads the sheets with the `DBZ_ASSETS_TOKEN` repository secret (Settings > Secrets and variables > Actions): set it to a token that can read dbz-assets.

## Online 1v1

Two players can fight each other over the network through a small lobby server.

1. Start the server on a computer both players can reach (standard library only, no install needed):
   `$ python3 server/lobby_server.py` (listens on port 5050; `--port` to change it).
2. In the game, choose **ONLINE 1V1** in the main menu (or press **O**), then pick your character.
3. In the lobby, check the server address at the top. Tap it (or press **E**) to change it: on the same computer `127.0.0.1:5050`, from a phone or another PC the server computer's address on your network, for example `192.168.1.20:5050`. The address is remembered. The default comes from `server` in `Config/config.json`.
4. One player taps **Create room** (or **C**), the other taps the room in the list (or presses its number). The match starts on its own.

The match takes place in front of Goku's house with all the usual moves. The first player knocked out loses, then both go back to the lobby. Online, each hit counts for 40% so matches last. The server has no accounts or encryption: run it on your own network, or on a server you control.

## Game Mechanics

### Controls

- **Choose your team**: After Start, pick your character (Goku, Vegeta, Gohan, Trunks or Broly), then up to 3 **allies** who fight on your side (or none, to fight alone), then up to 4 **opponents** (shown with how hard they are): anything from 1v1 to 4v4, 4v1 or 1v4, up to 8 fighters on the field. Tap a card or press Space to pick or unpick it, Enter or the button under the cards to go on. The **Back** button (top left) or Esc goes back one screen. Online matches skip the allies and opponents (the opponent is the other player).
- **Health bars**: Your HP and KI are top left, with one row per ally under them (blue names); the enemies (or your online opponent) are top right, under the quit button, their names in the colour of their difficulty (Hard in red). A row's bar gets a gold frame while that fighter is a Super Saiyan, and turns grey with "KO" once they are down. Over each fighter's head a thin bar, blue for allies and red for enemies, shows who is who in a crowd.
- **Allies and enemies**: The enemies spread along their patrol route and fight the closest of you and your allies; your allies start next to you, follow you when there is nobody to fight, and attack the closest enemy. Your punches and techniques never hit your allies, and an enemy's never hits another enemy. Allies and enemies also use their **special techniques** now and then (when the target is in line, close enough, and they have the ki), not just punches; harder enemies use them more often and harder.
- **End of the fight**: When you are knocked out, **You lost** offers **Retry** (Enter: the same fight again, same team) or **Main menu** (Esc). When every enemy is down, **Victory!** offers **Play again** or **Main menu**. The fight is frozen behind the choice.
- **Character stats**: Every character has its own strength, whoever plays it (you, an ally, an enemy, or the other player online), in `Config/stats.json`: **power** (damage of punches and techniques), **hp**, **defense** (damage taken is divided by it) and **speed**, relative to an average fighter (Gohan, 1.0). Broly 1.3 power and HP, 1.15 defense but slower (0.9); Goku 1.15 / 1.1 / 1.05 / 1.05; Vegeta 1.12 / 1.05 / 1.05 / 1.05; Gohan 1.0; Trunks 0.95 power and HP but the fastest (1.1). An enemy's difficulty applies on top of its stats.
- **Enemy difficulty**: Each enemy has a difficulty, set in `Config/enemies.json` with its lines. A difficulty scales the enemy's HP, damage, toughness, reaction time, attack pace, guard, speed and when it powers up. **Easy** is weaker and slower; **Normal** is the usual enemy; **Hard** has 1.8x HP, hits 2.5x harder, takes 40% less damage, reacts and strikes faster, walks faster and powers up sooner. Broly is Hard: punches alone won't beat him, so transform, use techniques and guard. Change an enemy's `"difficulty"` there, or tune the numbers of a difficulty.
- **Movement**: Use the arrow keys, or drag the on-screen analog stick (bottom left) on a touch device. The stick moves in any direction, not just eight.
- **Running**: Hold Space while moving, or push the stick past its inner ring.
- **Attacking**: Press **A** or the on-screen **AUTO** button. Each press is one move of the combo: a punch, another punch, a kick, another kick, then round again (holding does not repeat). You and the enemies strike at the same pace, at most once every 0.4 seconds, so mash the button to keep the combo going.
- **Guard**: Hold **F** or the on-screen **DEFENSE** button. Your character takes the guard pose: you take 70% less damage and still get pushed back a little, but you aren't interrupted; you can't move or attack while guarding.
- **Knockback**: Every hit pushes the one who takes it a little away from the attacker, for you and the enemy alike. Both have the same reach, and an enemy waits a moment after stepping in before striking, so you can hit first.
- **Special techniques**: Press **D** or the on-screen blast button to use the selected technique; it costs KI. Choose it with the small arrows on top of that button, or **Q** / **E**; its name and cost show under the KI bar. Each character strikes its own poses for every form; the effects (blasts, beams) come from the spritedatabase.net sheets, and for Trunks and Broly from the effects sheet of dbz-assets:

  | Character | Techniques |
  |---|---|
  | Goku | Energy Blast (6 KI), Kamehameha (18 KI), Instant Transmission (12 KI: reappears next to the enemy and strikes) |
  | Gohan | Energy Blast (6 KI), Kamehameha (18 KI), Ki Burst (15 KI: a shockwave hitting everyone close) |
  | Vegeta | Energy Blast (6 KI), Final Flash (18 KI), Big Bang Attack (20 KI) |
  | Trunks | Energy Blast (6 KI), Burning Attack (20 KI), Sword Slash (15 KI: a slash hitting everyone close) |
  | Broly | Energy Blast (6 KI), Eraser Cannon (20 KI), Blaster Shell (15 KI: a shockwave hitting everyone close) |

  **Charging:** Kamehameha, Final Flash, Big Bang Attack, Burning Attack and Eraser Cannon charge while you hold the button (or **D**): a ki ball grows in the hands and a bar fills above the head. Let go to fire; at full charge (1.5 s) it fires by itself. The longer the charge, the bigger and stronger the shot (from half damage at a tap to full damage). Getting hit while charging cancels it.

  The techniques and the sprite boxes they use are listed in `Config/characters/<name>/specials.json`.
- **Super Saiyan**: Every character but Broly (already the Legendary Super Saiyan, and bigger than everyone) can transform. Press **S** or the on-screen transform button (Goku powering up) to transform (needs 20 KI), and again to go back. While transformed you move 1.5x faster, deal twice the damage (punches, kicks and techniques) and take half, but KI slowly drains (1 per second, about 100 seconds on a full bar) instead of regenerating; you revert when it runs out.
- **Super Saiyan 3** (Goku only): pick the level with **Z / X** or the small arrows above the transform button (the label shows SSJ or SSJ3), then press **S**. SSJ3 needs 35 KI, deals 2.5x damage instead of 2x, fires a 1.4x bigger Kamehameha, and drains 2 KI per second instead of 1. While transformed you can pick the other level and press S to switch directly; press S at the current level to go back to base.
- **Talking to NPCs**: Press **T** while near an NPC (no on-screen button yet).
- **Full screen**: The game opens full screen at the display's own resolution (always on Android). On desktop, press **F11** to switch between full screen and a window, or set `"fullscreen": false` in `Config/config.json` to start in a window.
- **Quit or back to the menu**: Press **Esc** (or the Android back button), or tap the **X** button in the top-right corner. While playing it offers **Quit** (Enter), **Main menu** (M) or **No** (Esc); from the main menu it only asks whether to quit. While the question is open the game is paused.
- **Menu**: Select Start or Options with the keyboard or tap. Options shows the control guide; press Esc or tap to return.
- **Enemies**: Enemies are tough (they only take 60% of each hit); they flinch when hit but strike back as soon as they can, and guard after a few quick hits. The enemy (Gohan unless you choose another) powers up when his health drops to half (sooner on Hard): his bar's frame turns gold, he moves faster, hits 50% harder and takes half damage.
- **Combat resources**: The HUD shows HP and KI (mana). Punches and kicks are free; special techniques cost KI, which regenerates over time. Nearby hostile NPCs pursue and attack back.
